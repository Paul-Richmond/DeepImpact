Why cant we get such good shots of our Moon? - delfirbankio , Doncaster, United Kingdom, 18/3/2013 08:14+++++We have far better shots of the Moon than these photos of Rhea. Cassini made its fly-by of Rhea at an altitude of 620 miles and the maximun photographic resolution it achieved was 18 metres per pixel. The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter has mapped the entire Moon at a resolution of 50cm per pixel from its nominal mapping orbit of 50km (and has photographed some of the Apollo landing sites at a resolution of 25cm per pixel from a lower orbit). In other words, LRO has mapped the entire Moon with a thousand times more spatial resolution data per unit area than these photos of Rhea. The 25cm per pixel photos of the Apollo sites have more than five thousand times the data.
