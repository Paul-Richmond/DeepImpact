    Oxygen is a very reactive element. Chemical equilibrium would entail all (or most) oxygen being sequestered in oxides with other other elements.

    To find large-ish amounts of free elemental oxygen one would have to conclude that some mechanism was at work, pumping it into the atmosphere after removing it from its oxides.

    In short, free elemental oxygen as a discovery would be as surprising as finding, say elemental sodium lying around in fields - it's just too reactive to stay that way without constant renewal.
